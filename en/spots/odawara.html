How to find Atami & Sagami Bay

After Odawara, as Atami approaches, the window opens wide toward Sagami Bay. Hillside town, sea and headlands fold into one view: the moment the ride stops being a commute and starts being a journey. Look from Seat A.

More: Wikipedia: Atami / @letus10: Hatsushima window article (Japanese only) / @letus10: Atami Castle window article (Japanese only)

If you are traveling from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, start watching the Seat A · sea side window as you approach Odawara → Atami. If you are traveling toward Tokyo, the order is reversed.

How to catch Atami & Sagami Bay

1. Choose the window first

As you approach Odawara → Atami, start watching from Seat A · sea side. Use Live Guide while riding, or the map on this page before you board.

The view lasts roughly 2 minutes. Watch how it changes as the train approaches, not just the single photo moment.

2. What makes it worth watching

Water views are satisfying because the scenery suddenly opens up after dense urban sections. The surface can make the change noticeable even on cloudy days.

This spot has been checked through listed photos or ride logs. Reference links are included where available.
