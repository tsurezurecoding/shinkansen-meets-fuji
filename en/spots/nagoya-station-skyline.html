How to find Nagoya Station Skyline

On trains from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, the window suddenly turns urban just before arriving at Nagoya: high-rise buildings, dense station-front blocks, and layers of tracks. It is not a mountain or a castle, but it is an essential view that signals Nagoya is coming.

If you are traveling from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, start watching the Seat E · mountain side window as you approach Just before Nagoya. If you are traveling toward Tokyo, the order is reversed.

How to catch Nagoya Station Skyline

1. Choose the window first

As you approach Just before Nagoya, start watching from Seat E · mountain side. Use Live Guide while riding, or the map on this page before you board.

This wider view appears intermittently through the section. Buildings and terrain may block it, so the timing is a guide for when to start looking, not a continuous visibility claim.

2. Highlights

Hills and mountains layer the distant outline with the towns in front of them. Weather and season change the impression from ride to ride.
