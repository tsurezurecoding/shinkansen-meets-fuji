How to find Nagoya Station Skyline

Around Nagoya Station, the window suddenly becomes urban: high-rise buildings, dense station-front blocks, and layers of tracks. It is not a mountain or a castle, but it is an essential Tokaido Shinkansen view.

If you are traveling from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, start watching the Both sides near the station window as you approach Around Nagoya Station. If you are traveling toward Tokyo, the order is reversed.

Why this view matters

Nagoya Station Skyline is one of the window views that make the Tokaido Shinkansen more than a transfer. The train moves fast, so visibility depends on weather, seat position, and timing.
