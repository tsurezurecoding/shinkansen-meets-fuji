How to find Shimizu Port and CHIKYU

Between Shin-Fuji and Shizuoka, look from Seat A for Shimizu Port: gantry cranes, and sometimes the deep-sea drilling vessel CHIKYU. After Mt. Fuji, another kind of scale appears outside the window: mountains, sea, and industry all close together.

More: Port of Shimizu: Deep-sea drilling vessel CHIKYU

If you are traveling from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, start watching the Seat A · sea side window as you approach Shin-Fuji → Shizuoka. If you are traveling toward Tokyo, the order is reversed.

How to catch Shimizu Port and CHIKYU

1. Choose the window first

As you approach Shin-Fuji → Shizuoka, start watching from Seat A · sea side. Use Live Guide while riding, or the map on this page before you board.

The clearest view lasts only a few seconds. Decide the seat side and window direction before it arrives rather than reaching for your camera late.

2. Highlights

Water views are satisfying because the scenery suddenly opens up after dense urban sections. The surface can make the change noticeable even on cloudy days.
