How to find To-ji Pagoda

South of Kyoto Station rises the five-story pagoda of To-ji — the tallest wooden tower in Japan, watching over the city for 1,200 years. Catching it from the train window is the perfect announcement: you have arrived in Kyoto.

More: THE THOUSAND KYOTO: To-ji Temple

If you are traveling from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, start watching the Seat A · sea side window as you approach Kyoto Sta.. If you are traveling toward Tokyo, the order is reversed.

How to catch To-ji Pagoda

1. Choose the window first

As you approach Around Kyoto Sta., start watching from Seat A · sea side. Use Live Guide while riding, or the map on this page before you board.

The clearest view lasts only a few seconds. Decide the seat side and window direction before it arrives rather than reaching for your camera late.

2. Highlights

Historic buildings appear as brief markers inside the cityscape. The pleasure is less about a long panorama and more about catching the train's relationship with the town.
