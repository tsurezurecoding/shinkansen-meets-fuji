Left-Side Fuji

On the Tokaido Shinkansen, just after Shizuoka Station and the Abe River, Mt. Fuji briefly appears on the Seat A side — the famous 'Left-side Fuji.' Learn its historical background on the old Tokaido, when it is visible, and how to spot it in a few seconds from the train.
