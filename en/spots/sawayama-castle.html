How to find Sawayama Castle Ruins

Soon after Maibara, the hill and sign for Sawayama Castle may appear on the Seat E side. This was the castle of Ishida Mitsunari, defeated at Sekigahara. No keep remains, but spotting the sign connects Sekigahara, Sawayama, and Hikone in just a few minutes of window time.

More: @letus10: Sawayama and Hikone window article (Japanese only) / Hikone tourism: Sawayama Castle Ruins

If you are traveling from Tokyo toward Shin-Osaka, start watching the Seat E · mountain side window as you approach Maibara → Kyoto. If you are traveling toward Tokyo, the order is reversed.

How to catch Sawayama Castle Ruins

1. Choose the window first

As you approach Maibara → Kyoto, start watching from Seat E · mountain side. Use Live Guide while riding, or the map on this page before you board.

The clearest view lasts only a few seconds. Decide the seat side and window direction before it arrives rather than reaching for your camera late.

2. Highlights

Historic buildings appear as brief markers inside the cityscape. The pleasure is less about a long panorama and more about catching the train's relationship with the town.
