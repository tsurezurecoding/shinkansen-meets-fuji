Sawayama Castle Ruins

After Maibara, the dark green hill with a signpost visible from Seat E is Sawayama Castle Ruins — Ishida Mitsunari's stronghold before Sekigahara, dismantled by the Tokugawa side after his defeat. Learn its history and how to spot it from the Shinkansen.
