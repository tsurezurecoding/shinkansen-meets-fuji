01 / GODZILLA · Tokyo and the Tama River

Godzilla’s Tokyo: from city streets to the train window

After looking up at Godzilla in the city, look for the bridge from Shin Godzilla. Let your city walk continue through the train window.

01 Shinjuku

Above the buildings, look up

Walk along the street in Kabukicho, and Godzilla’s face and claws appear above the sign-covered buildings. This is the roughly 12-metre-tall Godzilla Head on the eighth floor of the Shinjuku Toho Building.

Include the street and its signs in your photo. People and buildings give Godzilla a sense of scale while capturing the energy of the neighbourhood.

Shinjuku Godzilla Head

Nearest station
About 5 minutes on foot from JR Shinjuku Station’s East Exit
Where to look
The street in front of the Shinjuku Toho Building
Performance
Hourly from noon to 8 p.m. (according to the venue)

The terrace is closed (checked November 10, 2026). You can look up at the Godzilla Head from street level.

02 Hibiya

This time, stand at the statue’s feet

At Hibiya Godzilla Square, you can see Godzilla from dorsal fins to tail. The fins and textured skin stand out under the night lighting, with a different impact from the face high above Shinjuku. There is another Godzilla statue inside the cinema beside the square.

A detour from Tokyo Station

The nearest stations are Hibiya on the subway and JR Yurakucho. Combine your walk with Ginza, another setting in Godzilla Minus One.

Map of Hibiya Godzilla Square ↗

That familiar theme, even on the platform

Listen while you wait on the subway platform. At Hibiya Station on the Hibiya and Chiyoda lines, the departure melody is the Godzilla theme.

03 Tokyo–Shinagawa

Between the buildings, Tokyo Tower

As the train leaves Tokyo for Shinagawa, look between the buildings on the E-seat side for the red-and-white tower struck by a heat ray in Godzilla: Tokyo SOS. As the train moves on, the tower slips behind the buildings again.

Tokyo Tower from the train →

A detour in Shinagawa

Walk to the original landing place

In the original Godzilla (1954), his first appearance on the mainland is near the eastern end of Yatsuyama Bridge in Shinagawa. In the film still, the train held in his mouth emphasises his enormous size. This is Tokyo before the Tokaido Shinkansen and Tokyo Tower.

The current bridge is the fourth, rebuilt in 1985. The Shinkansen enters a tunnel after Shinagawa. To reach the bridge, walkabout 9 minutes from JR Shinagawa Station, or about 4 minutes from Keikyu Kita-Shinagawa Station.

04 Tama River

Over the Tama River, those familiar blue arches

The Tama River is where the Self-Defense Forces confront Godzilla in Shin Godzilla (2016). The arches in front of Godzilla belong to Maruko Bridge. You can see the same bridge from a different angle through the Shinkansen window.

Shinagawa–Shin-Yokohama E-seat side

As you cross the Tama River, look for the arches stretching across the water.
