Forecast model beta: visibility chance = probability that the summit is not obscured by cloud × probability that atmospheric visibility reaches the effective viewing distance. Because the sightline rises diagonally through the haze layer toward the summit, the effective distance is shorter than the horizontal distance; higher humidity makes the haze layer thicker. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0 and is converted into our own probability estimate. In a retrospective check against window-photo records, the model classified 9 out of 10 successful photography days as visible. Passing times are approximate and based on Nozomi services. Sudden weather changes may make the forecast inaccurate. For ideas on cloudy days, see the Mt. Fuji FAQ.
