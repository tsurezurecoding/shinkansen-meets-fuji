THE BASICS

What to know before you watch

This is one specially painted train running between Tokyo and Shin-Osaka in regular published timetable slots.Its exterior features Disney characters dressed for the 25th anniversary of Tokyo DisneySea, with the park's eight themed ports behind them.For the complete design and latest operating days, see JR Central's official information.

This page is not an official guide. It organizes published operating patterns for window watching, then estimates when and where your selected train may meet it and which side to watch. Official information takes priority for operating days and times.

        READ BEFORE YOU WATCH

        About these estimates

        Times are estimates calculated by linearly interpolating station times between reference stations on the Tokaido Shinkansen. Use ±5 minutes as a guide and start looking out a little early.

        Station stops, track and speed operations, delays, and changes on the day can all change the view. If your train is part of the same special operation, the result says that your train is the special train rather than an encounter.

        We do not calculate unconfirmed dates or dates outside the operating period. For the latest dates, trains, and times, rely on the official sources below.
