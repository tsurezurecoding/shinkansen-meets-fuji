How to see Mt. Fuji from the Shinkansen

Seat side, timing, Left-Side Fuji, and other Tokaido Shinkansen window views.

Which side of the Shinkansen is Mt. Fuji on?

On the Tokaido Shinkansen, Mt. Fuji is mainly on the Seat E, mountain-side window in both directions. There is also a brief Left-Side Fuji moment near Shin-Fuji to Shizuoka, when Fuji can appear on Seat A. See Left-Side Fuji

When can you see Mt. Fuji from the Shinkansen?

The biggest view is around Mishima to Shin-Fuji. On clear days, you may also glimpse Fuji closer to Tokyo, around Shinagawa to Shin-Yokohama, or farther west near Lake Hamana. See the Mt. Fuji view

What else can you see from the Tokaido Shinkansen window?

Sagami Bay, Atami, Lake Hamana, castles, To-ji Temple, mountains, factories, signs, and other short-lived views make the ride itself part of the journey. Browse the field guide
